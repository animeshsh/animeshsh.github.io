Mathematical pipeline

Equations behind Stable Diffusion

Stable Diffusion performs diffusion in a compressed latent space. The equations below describe the main stages used during training and image generation.

1. Encode the image into latent space

\[z_0 = E(x), \qquad \tilde z_0=s\,z_0\]

The VAE encoder \(E\) compresses image \(x\) into latent \(z_0\). Stable Diffusion commonly applies a model-specific scale factor \(s\), giving the diffusion-model latent \(\tilde z_0\). The exact scaling convention depends on the model implementation.

2. Forward diffusion

\[q(\tilde z_t\mid \tilde z_0)=\mathcal{N}\!\left(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,\tilde z_0,\,(1-\bar\alpha_t)I\right)\]
\[\tilde z_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,\tilde z_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon,\qquad \epsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)\]

Known Gaussian noise is gradually added to the clean latent. Here \(\alpha_t=1-\beta_t\) and \(\bar\alpha_t=\prod_{i=1}^{t}\alpha_i\).

3. Noise-prediction training loss

\[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{simple}}=\mathbb{E}_{x,\epsilon,t,c}\left[\left\|\epsilon-\epsilon_\theta(\tilde z_t,t,c)\right\|_2^2\right]\]

The denoiser \(\epsilon_\theta\), usually a U-Net in classic Stable Diffusion, learns to predict the sampled noise at timestep \(t\), conditioned on text embedding \(c\).

4. Classifier-free guidance

\[\hat\epsilon_\theta=\epsilon_\theta(\tilde z_t,t,\varnothing)+w\left[\epsilon_\theta(\tilde z_t,t,c)-\epsilon_\theta(\tilde z_t,t,\varnothing)\right]\]

The guidance scale \(w\) amplifies the difference between conditional and unconditional predictions. Larger values generally increase prompt adherence.

5. One common DDPM reverse step

\[\tilde z_{t-1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}\left(\tilde z_t-\frac{1-\alpha_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}}\hat\epsilon_\theta(\tilde z_t,t,c)\right)+\sigma_t\eta\]

For an epsilon-prediction DDPM sampler, the scheduler uses the predicted noise to move from \(\tilde z_t\) toward a cleaner latent \(\tilde z_{t-1}\). Different samplers modify this update rule.

6. Cross-attention

\[Q=Z W_Q,\qquad K=C W_K,\qquad V=C W_V\]
\[\operatorname{Attention}(Q,K,V)=\operatorname{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V\]

Latent image features form the queries, while text-token embeddings provide keys and values. The attention matrix links words to spatial features.

7. Decode the final latent

\[\hat{x}=D\!\left(\frac{\tilde z_0}{s}\right)\]

After the final denoising step, the VAE decoder \(D\) converts the latent representation back into a visible RGB image.

8. Token influence for interpretability

\[I_j=\left\|\hat{x}(c)-\hat{x}(c_{\setminus j})\right\|\]

A simple token-ablation score compares the generated image with and without token \(j\). A larger difference suggests stronger causal influence.

\(x\): original image \(\tilde z_t\): scaled latent at timestep \(t\) \(c\): text-conditioning embedding \(\epsilon\): sampled Gaussian noise \(\epsilon_\theta\): U-Net noise predictor \(\beta_t\): noise schedule \(w\): guidance scale \(D,E\): VAE decoder and encoder
Worked numerical example

See the equations operate on one number

A real latent contains thousands of values. To make the arithmetic visible, this example treats the latent as one scalar value. The same operations are applied element-by-element to real latent tensors.

A. Add noise

\[\tilde z_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,\tilde z_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon\]

B. Apply classifier-free guidance

\[\hat\epsilon=\epsilon_u+w(\epsilon_c-\epsilon_u)\]

Guidance extrapolates from the unconditional prediction toward the text-conditioned prediction. It does not directly multiply the image.

C. Take one DDPM denoising step

\[\mu_\theta=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}\left(\tilde z_t-\frac{\beta_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}}\hat\epsilon\right)\]

Using \(\alpha_t=0.90\), \(\beta_t=0.10\), and no extra random term for clarity:

A full stochastic DDPM sampler then uses \(\tilde z_{t-1}=\mu_\theta+\sigma_t\eta\). Deterministic samplers such as DDIM use a related but different update.

Interpretability caveat: Attention maps are useful diagnostic signals, but they are not complete causal explanations. A real analysis should compare attention, gradients, latent interventions, token ablation, and output changes.
